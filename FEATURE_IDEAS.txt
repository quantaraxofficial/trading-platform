TradePilot - new feature ideas
==============================
Effort: S = a day or two, M = under a week, L = more than that.


MAKE MONEY / GROW
-----------------
1. Paid subscriptions (S-M)
   Pricing tiers already exist on the site, but nothing charges or enforces them.
   Connect a payment provider (Razorpay or Stripe) and lock features by plan
   (copy-trading followers, hidden-code scripts, more MT5 accounts).

2. Paid copy-trading channels and invite-only scripts (M)
   Channel owners set a monthly price; the site takes a cut. The Telegram signal
   group idea, automated end to end. Depends on #1.

3. Leaderboards (S)
   Top traders by verified MT5 or paper performance, plus top scripts and top
   channels. Brings traffic to profiles and channels.


TRUST
-----
4. Verified track records (M)
   A "Verified" badge on profiles and channels whose stats come straight from the
   linked MT5 history, not self-reported. The main thing buyers of signals want.

5. Two-factor sign-in and session list (S)
   Important now that real broker accounts are linked.


TRADING TOOLS
-------------
6. Trade replay inside Trade Analysis (M)
   The one TradeFXBook feature still missing: candle-by-candle replay around each
   trade's entry and exit, built on the existing bar replay.

7. Risk guard (M)
   Daily loss limit, maximum open risk and maximum lots across paper, MT5 and
   auto-trading. Blocks or warns before an order breaks your rules (prop-firm
   style rules).

8. Position-size calculator on the order ticket (S)
   Risk % plus a stop on the chart gives the lots, sized with the MT5 symbol's
   real tick value (the copier already has this logic).

9. Strategy optimizer (M-L)
   Runs a Pine strategy across ranges of its inputs, shows a heatmap of profit
   factor and drawdown, with a walk-forward check against overfitting. Reuses the
   Strategy Tester.

10. Alerts to Telegram and webhooks (S-M)
    Price, drawing, Pine alert() and copier events sent to Telegram or any
    webhook.


LEARNING AND COMMUNITY
----------------------
11. Ideas feed (M)
    Publish a chart snapshot with your analysis; people like and comment;
    followers see it in a feed (like TradingView's Ideas). Snapshots, profiles
    and follows already exist.

12. Journal reminders and weekly review (S)
    A Monday email or notification with last week's stats, best and worst trade,
    unjournaled trades and rule breaks. Reuses Analytics.

13. AI trade coach (M)
    Reads your Analytics and journal and points out patterns, e.g. "you lose most
    on Fridays after 3 PM" or "your losses grow after two wins".


PLATFORM
--------
14. Mobile layout (M-L)
    Phone-friendly chart, positions, copy-trading log and notifications, possibly
    an installable web app with push notifications.

15. Economic calendar on the chart (S-M)
    High-impact news as markers on the time axis, with an option to pause
    auto-trading or copying around news.


PROP FIRM CHALLENGES
--------------------
16. Prop firm rule guard (L)
    The trader picks their prop firm, the program (1-step / 2-step / instant) and the
    account size they bought. Before each trade the site warns them against that firm's
    rules ("today's loss limit leaves $340; this trade risks $410 at its stop loss -
    think again"), including the hidden rules buried in the terms and FAQ, so they never
    have to look the rules up again. Builds on Risk guard (#7) and the economic calendar
    (#15, for news-trading windows).

    There's no public API for prop firm rules: we gather them ourselves from each firm's
    own pages, as numbers the warnings can calculate with (not as text).

    a) What to store, per firm -> program -> account size
       Visible rules:
       - Profit target per phase, minimum trading days, time limit
       - Daily loss: the %, balance or equity based, whether floating losses count,
         reset time and timezone (firms differ most here; it fails the most traders)
       - Max loss: static or trailing; trails balance, equity or end of day; whether it
         stops trailing at the starting balance
       - Leverage per asset class, platform, price
       Hidden rules (mostly in the terms of service and the FAQ, not the sales page):
       - News trading: banned or not, the window (e.g. 2 or 5 minutes either side),
         which events, funded accounts only or not
       - Consistency: e.g. best day no more than X% of total profit
       - Max risk per trade or a lot-size cap
       - Stop loss required, or required within X minutes
       - Minimum trade duration (no trades closed within seconds)
       - Weekend / overnight holding
       - Bans on EAs, copy trading, high-frequency trading, hedging across accounts
       - "Gambling" clauses (e.g. all-in on one trade)
       - Inactivity limit, IP / VPN rules, payout conditions
       With every rule: the source link, the quoted source text, the date it took
       effect and the date it was last checked. The app shows the date and the link,
       so traders can trust the warning and check it themselves.

    b) Where it comes from
       - The firm's help centre / FAQ: most of the hidden rules, in tidy articles
       - The terms of service / trader agreement (often a PDF): the strictest rules
         (gambling, max risk, payout refusal)
       - The checkout page: account sizes, prices, programs on offer
       - The firm's Discord and emails: rule changes often appear there first
       - Our users: "this rule changed" reports, into a review queue
       - Comparison sites (PropFirmMatch and the like): to cross-check only; copying
         their database would likely break their terms

    c) How to keep it up to date (semi-automatic, never fully automatic)
       1. Start with about 10 of the most popular forex firms (FTMO, FundedNext,
          The5ers, FundingPips, E8 and similar), not every firm
       2. A scheduled job saves a copy of each firm's FAQ and terms pages and flags
          any page that changed
       3. Claude reads the changed page and fills in the fields above, quoting the
          source text for each one
       4. A person approves each change before it goes live: a wrong rule can cost a
          trader an account they paid for
       5. Prices and account sizes checked again every month

    d) What the trader adds
       - Firm, program, size and current phase
       - Starting balance and start date
       - Balance / equity at the daily limit's last reset
       Live equity comes from the linked MT5 account automatically; on paper trading
       the site already knows everything.

    e) The warnings
       Before a trade: the money at risk if the stop loss is hit, against what's left
       of today's loss limit (floating losses included where the firm counts them),
       what's left of the max loss, and the firm's risk-per-trade or lot caps.
       Also flagged: no stop loss, a news event inside the firm's window, a trade
       closed too quickly, the consistency limit getting close.
       Each warning notes that firms change their rules and links the firm's page.

    First step: draft the data structure and fill it in for 2-3 firms by reading
    their help centres and terms, to see how much of the hidden-rule detail can be
    captured reliably before building the warnings.


TOP PICKS, IN ORDER
-------------------
1. Subscriptions (#1) and paid channels (#2) - turn what's built into revenue.
2. Verified track records (#4) - make the channels sellable.
3. Trade replay (#6) - completes the Analytics comparison with TradeFXBook.
4. Risk guard (#7) - protects users now that real money is involved.
