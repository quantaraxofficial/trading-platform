1. DRAWING TOOLS (LEFT TOOLBAR)
======================================================================
Every tool was studied in TradingView Desktop (points, default levels, colours, look) and
added with TradingView's icons, order and shortcuts.

Lines
  - Ray, extended line, info line (price change, %, pips/ticks, bars, time span, angle),
    trend angle (arc with the angle in degrees)
  - Horizontal / vertical / cross line across the whole chart, labelled on the axes in the
    line's colour (Alt+H, Alt+V, Alt+C)

Fibonacci and Gann
  - Trend-based fib extension, fib channel, time zone, speed resistance fan, trend-based fib
    time, circles, spiral, speed resistance arcs, wedge, pitchfan
  - Gann box, Gann square fixed, Gann square, Gann fan

Patterns
  - XABCD, Cypher, Head and shoulders, ABCD, Triangle pattern, Three drives (leg ratios and
    labels)
  - Elliott waves: impulse, correction, triangle, double and triple combo
  - Cycles: cyclic lines, time cycles, sine line

Forecasting and measuring
  - Position forecast, bars pattern, ghost feed, sector
  - Anchored VWAP with band; fixed range and anchored volume profile (POC, 70% value area)
  - Price range, date range, date and price range

Channels and pitchforks
  - Parallel channel, regression trend (least squares, +/-2 SD, Pearson's R), flat
    top/bottom, disjoint channel
  - Pitchfork, Schiff, modified Schiff, inside pitchfork

Text, notes and content
  - Note, price note, pin, table (editable cells), callout, comment, price label, signpost,
    flag mark (start typing as soon as they're placed)
  - Image (file picker), post and idea cards

All tools share one multi-point shell (select, move, snapping handles) and feed the toolbar
flyouts and quick search.
  - Shapes can be selected, moved and middle-click deleted in Brave (and other browsers that
    add noise to canvas pixels): the shape under the pointer is found by its nearest colour
  - Clicking the middle of a line selects it (a second click on its label adds text)
  - Each tool's last-used style is saved in full to the account, so a reload keeps it
  - TradingView's Fib retracement as the new-user default (levels 0 to 4.236 in their
    colours, shaded); "Apply Default Drawing Template" in the Fib dialog and toolbar
  - Fixed range / anchored volume profile: TradingView's toolbar and settings (rows, volume
    up/down / total / delta, value area, width, placement, colours, VAH / VAL / POC,
    developing POC and VA)
  - Changing a rectangle's (circle's, ellipse's, arc's) border keeps its background colour
  - Highlighter: uncrowded toolbar, no dotted ring when selected; brush / highlighter
    strokes show in the last-used colour while drawn
  - Favourites bar: drag its tools to reorder them; it never covers the drawing toolbar
  - Colour palettes close on an outside click and apply shades as they're picked
  - Shape settings dialogs shrink to fit small windows; toolbar menus open where they fit
  - Rotated rectangle as on TradingView: clicks 1 and 2 set its centre line, click 3 its width
    (equal on both sides); six handles (the line's ends keep the width, the corners change
    only the width); TradingView's green outline and fill; the Background checkbox works
  - Brush, hovered or selected: hollow rings at its two ends, the stroke unchanged (no glow)
  - Filled shapes (rectangle, rotated rectangle, circle, ellipse, triangle, polyline, path,
    arc, curves) take the mouse only on their outline: inside them no hover handles, no
    "+ Add text", no selection, as on TradingView
  - Typing text in a shape (text, rectangle, circle, ellipse, lines, note, callout, table…) works
    as on TradingView: the shape draws the text as it's typed with a blinking caret and
    selection (an invisible input takes the keys), so it doesn't move when Enter / a click
    outside ends typing; a click in the text moves the caret; Escape takes the typing back
  - A trend line's text sits 7px clear above the line (TradingView's gap), more lines going up
  - A shape's "+ Add text" hint is in its text's own colour and size (a red line's is red), so
    it looks the same before, while and after typing
  - Points placed to the right of the latest candle land on the future bars' times
  - Dragged shapes follow the pointer without ever jumping back to where the drag began
    (the Long/Short position "dancing"); Long/Short moves are logged in the browser console
    (entry, target, stop, bars, times)
  - With many drawings only the drawing that changes is redrawn: the crosshair keeps up with
    the mouse and drags stay smooth (150 drawings: slowest mouse moves 240 ms -> 44 ms)
  - Mac: Command+drag box-selects drawings, and Command does the other Ctrl gestures
    (Ctrl+drag copy, magnet on/off); Windows keeps Ctrl
  - Chart right-click menu: "Remove N drawings", with the count

2. CHART, LAYOUTS AND CHART TYPES
======================================================================
  - An indicator turned off (its eye, on the chart or in the Object tree) stays off after a reload,
    for every indicator (EMA, Volume and the session range only hid it until the page reloaded)
  - The candle's countdown to close stays on top of the price axis's other labels (a selected
    drawing's or a position's labels no longer cover it)
Chart interactions
  - The cursor stays the cross over the whole chart (candles, the price "+" button, execution
    arrows); clicking a candle selects the price series (dots on the candle bodies)
  - Changing an EMA's settings redraws just that EMA at once; results are remembered (9 -> 15 -> 9)
  - Right-click menu on a candle: add alert / order / indicator, symbol info, copy price,
    paste, table view, add to watchlist, add text note, settings
  - Double-click a candle: Settings on the Symbol tab
  - Navigation buttons near the bottom: zoom in/out, scroll left/right, reset chart view
  - Switching timeframe keeps the candle size (the same number of bars across), at the live
    edge showing the new timeframe's newest bars, as on TradingView
  - Smooth zoom and pan, as on openmarket.xyz: wheel zoom / pan glide to where the wheel is
    taking them (eased ~140 ms, TradingView's anchoring and step), the price axis glides to its
    new range instead of snapping, and a released drag keeps sliding (momentum)
  - Wheel zoom after the price axis was dragged (autoscale off) still follows the bars: the
    axis keeps its stretch and shift against the visible high / low instead of freezing
    (normal scale; a log scale keeps its range)
  - Symbol info dialog: exchange, currency, tick size, today's session on a 24-hour bar
  - Vertical grid lines carry on to the right of the latest candle, at the chart's own spacing
  - Settings -> Canvas text colour also colours the legend (title, Vol, indicator names; their
    inputs a faded shade)
  - Countdown to bar close readable on light labels (black text with white bullish candles)
  - The crosshair pointer is an image, so viewers see it when the chart tab is shared in
    Google Meet (Chrome leaves the system crosshair out of a shared tab)
  - Dialogs opened from the chart (strategy / indicator settings, alerts, volume, session,
    symbol info, save template) open above the Strategy Tester and other panels instead of
    being cut off by them
  - Script settings (a strategy's or a Pine indicator's Inputs / Properties / Style) laid out
    like the other settings dialogs, as on TradingView: label left, control right, group
    headings, TradingView checkboxes, colour boxes, a Defaults menu (Reset settings)
  - Script settings apply as they're changed: the chart and the Strategy Tester report re-run
    behind the open dialog; Cancel / Esc put the opening settings back, Ok keeps them
  - Strategy Tester custom date range can start before the chart's first loaded bar (e.g.
    January 2025 on a 2h chart); the older bars are loaded for the backtest
  - A custom testing period shows as picked; when it reaches before the data there is, a dialog
    says how much data there is (from / to, years, bars on the timeframe) or that the backtest's
    loading limit was reached, and offers to test the covered period
  - Backtest fills as TradingView's broker emulator: strategy(process_orders_on_close = true)
    fills market entries at the signal bar's close; when a stop and a limit both lie inside one
    bar, the one met first on TradingView's assumed path (open -> nearer extreme -> other) fills
  - Settings templates for indicators and strategies, as TradingView's Defaults menu: "Save as..."
    a named set of a script's inputs (and a strategy's initial capital, the plots' colours), pick
    one to apply it (the report re-runs live), delete it, "Save as default" (used whenever the
    script is added), "Reset settings". Kept in the account (database), so they follow the user;
    a custom script has to be saved on the site, or published publicly or privately, first
  - Indicator templates also hold built-in Pine indicators' inputs and the custom script /
    strategy on the chart with its settings (by where it's saved or published on the site);
    applying the template puts it back on the chart with those settings
  - Indicator and strategy settings dialogs (scripts, EMA, Volume) move by their title bar, as on
    TradingView, and stay reachable on screen
  - Symbol logos as TradingView's: a forex pair as its two round flags overlapping (USD, EUR,
    GBP, JPY, CHF, CAD, AUD, NZD, INR, MXN), gold / silver as a gold / silver coin with the
    quote's flag, BTC / ETH as their coins (watchlist, legend, trading panels)
  - The tab title's change % is since the previous day's close (it compared with the bar before
    and showed +0.00% on minute charts)

Layouts
  - The tab title shows the price to 3 decimals at least (the symbol's own if more), as
    TradingView's: "XAUUSD 3,945.400 ▼ −1.06%"
  - Multi-chart layouts (Layout setup grid, Manage layouts, sync in layout); each chart keeps
    its own chart type
  - Price scale on the left (Move scale to left)
  - Object tree and Data window: symbol, indicators and drawings, groups, drag to reorder,
    right-click menus, Manage layout drawings

Chart types
  - Every TradingView chart type, with favourites and per-type style settings
  - Renko, Line break, Kagi, Point & figure and Range built from the time bars

Other
  - Execution marks: thin arrows, hover pill, fills card
  - Symbol search: "xauusd" finds XAU/USD; Enter opens the top match
  - Space / Shift+Space step through the watchlist
  - Bar times in real UTC, so gold candles line up with TradingView
  - Symbols shown as TradingView writes them (XAUUSD, EURUSD) everywhere

Redesign (the site's own layout)
  - Top bar -> Redesign: the top bar, drawing toolbar, right panel, right toolbar and bottom
    panel (date ranges, Strategy Tester, paper trading) float with a shining blue border; drag
    any of them anywhere, resize the ones that can be resized by their edge handles
  - Magnetic: edges snap to the screen's and to the other widgets' (4px apart or lined up),
    with a guide line where they snapped
  - The chart takes the space left: a tall widget keeps its side of the screen, a wide one
    the top or bottom; small ones float over the chart
  - Done keeps the layout, Cancel / Esc puts it back, Reset returns to the default grid;
    the layout follows the account and adapts to other screen sizes (docked edges stay docked)
  - Settings -> Layout (new tab): default grid or custom layout, Redesign..., layout templates
    (save, apply, rename, delete; also "Save as template" in Redesign), and each widget's
    colour; applied live, Cancel reverts

Small screens and browsers
  - Drawing toolbar and right toolbar scroll on their own on short windows (the page
    doesn't); their flyout menus stay visible and fit the window
  - TradingView-style dropdowns in place of the browser's native lists, everywhere
  - Tested in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave and Safari's engine (WebKit)

4. TRADING: PAPER TRADING AND METATRADER 5
======================================================================
Paper trading

MetaTrader 5
  - Link an MT5 account (number, password, server; password stored encrypted)
  - Live balance, equity, margin, positions, pending orders, last 24 hours' deals in the
    trading panel
  - Orders: market, limit and stop with SL / TP, sized by the broker's rules; close all or

Auto-trading

    sells, flat closes, a reversal closes and opens
  - Once per signal, only its own tagged positions; Algo tab with the log
  - Strategies recalculate on each bar close

6. PINE EDITOR AND SCRIPTING
======================================================================
Pine engine
  - switch statements; about 50 ta.* functions plus math.*, str.*, color.*, matched against
    17 TradingView indicators (RSI to ~3e-6)
  - label / line / box drawings, user-defined types, methods, matrices, typed arrays,
    for...in, request.security of a higher timeframe, runtime.error
  - Indicators in their own pane (hlines, fills, gradients, shapes, per-bar colours)
  - Scripts run in a background worker; unknown functions give a clear error

Three languages
  - Choose Pine Script, Python or MQL5 beside the editor title
  - Python: CPython with NumPy in a sandboxed worker, Pine-like API, print() to Logs
  - MQL5: indicators with buffers and built-in handles (iMA, iRSI, iATR, iBands...); Expert
    Advisors trade via CTrade / OrderSend
  - The legend, settings dialog and Strategy Tester work the same for all three

Editor
  - Never loses keystrokes when typing fast
  - Collaborate: share only the script and edit it live together (cursors, chat, voice)
  - GitHub: connect with a token (stored encrypted), pull a file, push as a commit (branch,
    path, message), linked file for one-click pull / push, conflict check
  - Publish script (see Community)
  - Optimize button for strategies (see section 8)

8. STRATEGY TESTER AND STRATEGY OPTIMIZER
======================================================================
Strategy Tester
  - "Save to Analytics" keeps a backtest in the account

Strategy optimizer (Pine Editor -> Optimize; strategies only, all three languages)
  - Vary symbols, chart timeframes and the script's inputs: number ranges (from / to / step),
    on/off switches, option lists, timeframe inputs
  - Settings: testing period (one chart load, last N days, date range, whole history),
    initial capital, every combination or a random sample (up to 100,000), and a pace
    (Gentle / Balanced / Full speed)
  - Each combination is backtested in a background worker; the page stays responsive
  - Ranked by any of 18 metrics: net profit, net %, profit factor, return / drawdown,
    Sharpe, Sortino, win rate, profitable months, worst month, max drawdown, trades, average
    trade, long / short P&L...
  - Filters: fewest trades, deepest drawdown, lowest profit factor, profitable only,
    profitable every year
  - Pause, close the window or the page, continue later from the next combination
    (results are saved in the database as they're tested)
  - One window runs it at a time; another window sees "Running in another window" and can
    take it over
  - Settings are locked while running; changing them while paused starts it over (asks
    first); it keeps the code version it started with and offers to restart with new code
  - Report in its own bottom-panel tab, like the Strategy Tester:
      Summary: progress, the best combination with its full metric sheet, runners-up
      All results: sortable, filterable table
      Pause / Continue, collapse, maximize, close; stays across reloads
  - Apply to chart: switches symbol and timeframe and runs the strategy with those settings
    (the Strategy Tester then shows exactly the optimizer's figures)
  - Output -> "Download each combination's trades (.csv)": every combination tested saves its
    list of trades (the Strategy Tester's CSV) to the Downloads folder, one file each, named
    after the script, symbol, timeframe and settings; can be switched on or off while paused
    without starting the optimization over

11. ANALYTICS AND BACKTEST SESSIONS
======================================================================
Analytics page (/analytics, Products -> Analytics; TradePilot's look)
  - Performance: P&L, win rate, profit factor, expectancy, equity and drawdown curve, long vs
    short, weekdays, sessions, top symbols, daily calendar, full stats

Backtest sessions
  - Bar replays you come back to, with their own account, trades and drawings
  - Resume on the exact bar, timeframe and zoom; autosave; time spent; active / paused /
    completed
  - Drawings are separate per session; they never load into or save over the wrong symbol
    or session
  - Optional length when creating one ("how much to backtest": hours / days / weeks / months /
    years); the end bar is worked out (calendar months / years) and what's left shows with a
    progress bar ("2 months 5 days left of 3 months")
  - Trading in a session needs no broker connection; picking another bar keeps the session
    (its account and trades carry on); Analytics shows its open positions and working orders and
    follows it live
  - Backtest sessions panel in the right toolbar: every session with its status, bar, P&L,
    trades, time spent and time left; one clicked opens in a new tab and carries on
  - A reopened backtest session shows its last played candle
  - New session: the symbol is a dropdown by market (plus your watchlists, or type one)

12. COPY TRADING
======================================================================
Copier (Analytics -> Copy trading; Products -> Copy trading)
  - Run a channel: join code, default risk %, max slippage; send signals from the site or
    mirror your own MT5 trades (opens, stop changes, partial and full closes)
  - Follow channels with your own limits: signal's or own risk %, max risk, open copied
    trades, daily loss limit, symbols, lots for signals without a stop
  - Copies go straight to each follower's broker: the broker's symbol name, stops at the same
    distances from the fill, lots = risk % of the follower's own equity at that stop, late
    copies skipped, only tagged copies touched, every result logged with its latency
  - manage.py run_copier: one always-logged-in MT5 terminal per follower, each in its own
    process, so a signal reaches every account at once

Posting signals out
  - A channel posts every open, stop move and close to:
      Telegram channel / group (your own bot; moves and closes reply to the trade's post)
      Discord channel (webhook)
      WhatsApp numbers (Cloud API; approved template for business-initiated messages)
      your group chats, personal chats and collabs here
  - In chats here: a signal card with "Copy this channel", which follows the channel
  - Each connection is checked when saved, can be tested, paused, set per event (opens /
    stop moves / closes, join code on or off) and shows its last result
  - Tokens stored encrypted, never sent back; posting runs in the background

16. VOICE ASSISTANT
======================================================================
  - Right toolbar -> microphone icon: talk to the site (signed in). Everything said goes to an
    AI on the server (Groq, or Gemini as backup), which answers, asks when it needs to know
    more, and tells the site what to do; no fixed phrases. It knows the chart's symbol,
    timeframe and price and the conversation so far
  - It asks instead of guessing: "create a backtesting session" -> "From which date should it
    start?"; "mark the zones" -> "Which zones?"; the answer carries on the same request
  - Bigger actions wait for a yes: creating a backtest session ("Create a backtest on XAUUSD
    15m from 1 March 2024 with 10,000 dollars? Say yes or no") and clearing all the marks; say
    yes / no or press the Yes / No buttons
  - What it can do: open charts and timeframes; mark order blocks, fair value gaps, inverse
    FVGs, support and resistance (worked out from the chart's own bars, what's on screen plus
    150 bars back, drawn as labelled zones running to the latest bar, only zones still in play;
    one Object tree group per kind, marking again replaces it, Ctrl+Z undoes a marking) and
    clear them; add / remove indicators (RSI, MACD, ATR, EMA, SMA, VWAP, Ichimoku, pivots,
    volume profile...); chart type; site theme; bar replay from a date or a random one; a new
    alert at a price (opened to confirm); backtest sessions with symbol, timeframe, start
    date, balance and length, opened on the chart; chart image; reset view; Pine Editor;
    the right-hand panels; the price; stop listening. Up to three at once
  - Collabs by voice: "start a collab" asks for its name and who to invite (editor or viewer),
    or "defaults" (named after the symbol, nobody invited, people may ask to join, everyone's
    mic on); a name that matches several people is asked about ("Which Rahul?"); then a yes /
    no, and it's created, opened and the people invited. Also: invite people to the open
    collab (asked first), copy its link
  - The whole site by voice: every Quick search (Ctrl+K) function and the site's other controls

18. RUNNING IT (SETUP NOTES)
======================================================================
  - Database: run "python manage.py migrate" (migrations up to 0049)
  - Live collab / chats: the backend runs under daphne (Django Channels)
  - Copy trading: run "python manage.py run_copier" on the copier server (MT5 installed)
  - Market data: TWELVEDATA_API_KEY (8 requests a minute on the free plan; the optimizer
    waits these out automatically)
  - Voice assistant AI: set GROQ_API_KEY (free at console.groq.com) and / or GEMINI_API_KEY (free
    at aistudio.google.com) in backend/quantarax_backend/.env; optional GROQ_MODEL,
    GEMINI_MODEL, VOICE_AI_DAILY_LIMIT (default 100 per user per day). Without a key the
    voice assistant can't answer
  - cTrader accounts: register an application at openapi.ctrader.com (with a cTrader ID) and set
    CTRADER_CLIENT_ID, CTRADER_CLIENT_SECRET and CTRADER_REDIRECT_URI (the site's
    https://<site>/analytics/ctrader, also entered as the application's redirect URI) in
    backend/quantarax_backend/.env. Without them "Connect with cTrader" says it isn't set up
  - Feature ideas for later are listed in FEATURE_IDEAS.txt
