TRADEPILOT VOICE ASSISTANT: EVERYTHING YOU CAN SAY
==================================================

Open it with the microphone button in the right toolbar, then tap the orb to start (or stop)
listening: it grows and ripples as you speak, pulses as it answers, spins while it thinks. Speak or
type; any of the 27 languages in its language box works. The AI understands what you mean, so these are examples, not exact
wordings. It does up to 8 things from one sentence, in order ("switch to euro hourly, mark the
order blocks and add RSI"). Big actions ask "yes or no" first. If something needed is missing, it
asks. Anything about trading it can't do yet is noted for the team (Django Admin: voice requests).
This file is kept up to date: every new voice feature is added here.

Instant commands: everyday commands said plainly in English are understood on the site's own
server at once, with no AI call. They work even when the AI is busy or down, and don't use up the
day's AI answers. Examples: "mark order blocks and FVGs", "show me fresh order blocks", "find
setups", "clear the FVGs", "switch to 15 minutes", "daily", "open gold", "show me euro dollar on the
15 minute chart", "add RSI", "remove the EMA", "switch to Heikin Ashi", "dark mode", "open the
watchlist", "undo", "take a snapshot", "what's the price", "back to the chart", "guide me through your
website". Anything more goes
to the AI as before: a second instruction, a question, a date, other wording, or another language.

1b. WHAT A MARKET DID (ANY SYMBOL)
---------------------------------
Looked up in the symbol's own candles. Times are your own (your computer's time zone).
- A moment: "what was gold at 10:30 on 5 March". You get the price then, that candle's open / high /
  low / close, and the day's range. If the market was closed, the last price before.
- A period: "how did EUR/USD move last week", "what did Apple do in October", "gold over the last 3
  months", "Bitcoin from 1 to 10 March". You get:
  - the change, and the high and low with their times;
  - the path: its legs, e.g. "a brief rise, then a much bigger fall: up 2.03% to 10 Nov, then down
    7.13%";
  - the biggest single candles, and volatility against the period before;
  - participation (the crowd behind it): volume against the usual, growing or fading, how much of it
    came on rising vs falling candles (buyers or sellers in control), and its heaviest bursts. Gold's
    is our feed's tick volume; stocks have real volume; if a feed has no volume, it says so.


1c. SKETCH BOARD: MADE-UP CANDLES TO EXPLAIN WITH
-------------------------------------------------
For teaching: candles drawn on a blank chart (SKETCH), not a real market.
- Candles as you say them: "draw a big green candle, a small red one with a long top wick, then a
  green one". Sizes: tiny, small, medium, big, huge. Wicks: none, short, long, long top, long bottom.
- A trend: "make 20 candles in an uptrend, the green ones bigger", "a downtrend where red is bigger",
  "sideways candles".
- A pattern, its parts labelled. "Show me a double top", "explain a liquidity sweep with a drawing",
  "draw a bullish engulfing after a downtrend". The patterns:
  - candles: doji, hammer, shooting star, pin bar, bullish / bearish engulfing, morning / evening
    star, three white soldiers, three black crows, inside bar;
  - chart patterns: double top / bottom, head and shoulders (and inverse), ascending / descending
    triangle, bull / bear flag;
  - SMC: liquidity sweep (bullish / bearish), bullish / bearish FVG, bullish / bearish order block,
    BOS, CHoCH;
  - price action: range, breakout, V reversal, support, resistance.
- Everything works on it: "now mark the order blocks", any drawing tool.
- "Back to the chart" returns to the market you were on.


2. COPY A CHART FROM A PICTURE
------------------------------
Send a screenshot (the picture button by the text box, paste it, or drop it on the panel), then:
- "Make my chart look like this", "use this theme", "same colours as my TradingView".
  Copies the background (solid / gradient), grid, scale text and lines, the candles' body / border /
  wick colours, and the light / dark page.
- "Only the candle colours", "only the background".
- "Copy my drawings", "copy my fib", "copy my positions". Copied:
  - Fibs (each level's colour, the shading, the trend line);
  - trend lines, horizontal lines and rays, vertical lines, cross lines;
  - rectangles, triangles, circles, ellipses (outline and see-through fill);
  - long and short positions (entry, target, stop);
  - parallel channels, arrows, paths, polylines, brush strokes;
  - price / date ranges, up / down arrow marks.
  They're placed at the same candles and prices. If the picture is another timeframe, the chart
  switches to it. Their looks become the tools' defaults. Text drawings aren't copied: words can't
  be read from a picture.
- It asks "Keep it?": "no" puts everything back.

5. ANALYSIS: MARKING THE CHART FROM ITS BARS
--------------------------------------------
"Mark ..." analyses the bars on screen and draws the result. "Clear the marks" (or "clear the
order blocks") removes them.

SMC / ICT:
- Order blocks, fair value gaps (FVG), inverse FVGs (IFVG), BOS / CHoCH (market structure).
- POIs, buy-side / sell-side liquidity (EQH / EQL), liquidity sweeps / stop hunts.
- Premium / discount with OTE, breaker blocks, mitigation blocks, balanced price ranges (BPR).
- Displacement, kill zones / sessions, key levels (PDH / PDL, PWH / PWL, daily open).
- "Mark all SMC concepts".
- Order blocks, FVGs, IFVGs and POIs show their state (fresh, tapped ×N, past 50%,
  broken / filled):
  - "only fresh order blocks", "tapped FVGs", "all IFVGs including the broken ones".

Price action:
- Support and resistance, swing points (HH HL LH LL), trend lines, supply and demand zones.
- Candlestick patterns: all, or only some ("mark the dojis", "pin bars", "engulfing").
  The patterns: abandoned baby, belt hold, dark cloud cover, doji (dragonfly / gravestone /
  long-legged, doji star), engulfing, evening / morning star, hammer, hanging man, harami
  (and cross), inside / outside bar, inverted hammer, kicker, marubozu, piercing line, shooting
  star, spinning top, three black crows, three white soldiers, three inside / outside, three
  methods, tweezer.
- Chart patterns (double top / bottom, head and shoulders with necklines), ranges / consolidation.
- "Mark all price action".

MSNR (Malaysian SNR):
- A / V levels (fresh / unfresh), gap levels, SBR / RBS flips, QM / Quasimodo, compression.
- "Mark all MSNR".

Liquidity:
- Session highs and lows (Asia, London, New York, PMH / PML), inducement (IDM).
- Trend line liquidity, liquidity voids, round numbers.
- Draw on liquidity (ERL / IRL, "where is price going").
- "Mark all liquidity".

Trade setups:
- "Find setups", "any trades?", "show me Silver Bullets", "MSNR setups". Found as complete trades
  (entry, stop, target), drawn as positions and followed to now (won, lost, in trade, waiting,
  missed).
- ICT: Silver Bullet, Unicorn, Judas swing, OTE, Turtle soup, ICT 2022 model.
- SMC: CHoCH + OB, BOS + OB pullback, Sweep + CHoCH.
- Price action: pin bar at S/R, engulfing at S/R, break and retest, inside bar breakout,
  false breakout, double top / bottom, opening range breakout, flag, Wyckoff spring / upthrust.
- MSNR: fresh rejection, CP into level, SBR / RBS retest, QM.
- Liquidity: EQH / EQL sweep, IDM + POI, London sweep in the NY open.

Bias:
- "Analyse the last hour and predict the next hour", "bias for the next 4 hours", "today's bias".
- It reads the last window (move, close, sweep / break, structure on two timeframes, premium /
  discount), gives the lean with how often such reads were right on this market (tested on its
  history), a target, where it's wrong and a usual move, and draws it.
- "Clear the bias" removes the drawing.

Knowledge:
- Ask about any candlestick or chart pattern, SMC / ICT, price action, MSNR, liquidity or setup:
  "what is a breaker block", "how do I trade the silver bullet".
